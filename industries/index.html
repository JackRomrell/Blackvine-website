The Same Backend Problem Shows Up in Different Ways

Most high-ticket service businesses already have valuable opportunities inside calls, forms, consultations, estimates, appointment history, treatment interest, past customer lists, and inactive records. The details vary by industry, but the core problem is usually the same: opportunities need clearer follow-up, rebooking, reactivation, and tracking. That is why the follow-up system should be built around the industry, not applied generically.
