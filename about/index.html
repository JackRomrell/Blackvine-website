Founders

Jack Romrell

Co-Founder & Revenue Infrastructure Strategist

Jack previously operated a service-based business for several years, which gave him firsthand experience with what happens to a lead after it is generated — inconsistent follow-up, poorly organized contact records, missed opportunities, inactive contacts, weak attribution, and revenue slipping through gaps in the backend system.

Core Positioning

What Blackvine Media Is — and Isn’t

Blackvine Media helps high-ticket service businesses improve the systems between initial inquiry, lead capture, CRM entry, staff response, follow-up, appointment booking, appointment reminders, service delivery, rebooking, reactivation, and revenue attribution. We stay positioned around revenue infrastructure — not general marketing.

Not a Generic Marketing Agency

We don’t run general digital marketing or social media management as a stand-alone service.

Not a Bulk Email-Blast Company

Our email and SMS work is built into structured, approved workflows — not one-off blasts.

Not a Proprietary Software Company

We don’t sell our own CRM or automation platform. See Technology for the platforms we build on.

Not a CRM Reseller

We evaluate the systems you already use before recommending a change.

Not a Lead-Gen-Only Company

We don’t generate leads and hand them off without a backend follow-up system.

Revenue Infrastructure, Specifically

CRM organization, email and SMS automation, missed-call response, reminders, rebooking, reactivation, and tracking — built together.
