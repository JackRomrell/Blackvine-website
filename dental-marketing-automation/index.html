Dental Follow-Up Leaks We Help Fix

Most dental practices already have valuable opportunities inside their phones, forms, schedules, treatment plans, and patient lists. The problem is that those opportunities are not always followed up with consistently.

01

Missed Calls

High-intent new patients often call while staff are busy, and many do not wait long before calling another office.

02
